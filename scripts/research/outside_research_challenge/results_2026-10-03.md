# Positioning kill-tests — 2026-10-03 (daily Trade Idea session, second sweep)

Method identical to `trend_vs_positioning.py` (expanding percentiles, min 156
weeks, independent episodes 13 clear weeks apart, forward 63 sessions from
report date + 3, trend split on the 200-day). Data: `cot_extra_2026-10-03.json`
(pulled through the database http extension because the session egress blocks
publicreporting.cftc.gov). Prices: MacroTilt indicator_history.json.

## 1. Sterling — speculators at a record short is NOT a buy signal

Signal: legacy speculators net/OI <= 15th expanding percentile -> long GBP/USD.
Current: net -36.2% of OI (Sep 29 report, published Oct 2) = 5.1st percentile
of 1,344 weeks since 2001.

| | mean 63s | median | win | n |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conditional | -0.02% | +0.06% | 50% | 12 episodes, 2004-2026 |
| Unconditional | -0.22% | +0.11% | 51% | 1,142 |

No edge. 11 of 12 episodes came with price below its 200-day; those lost on
average. Worst episode -6.3% (the Brexit referendum sat inside the window).
Same verdict as the euro on 2026-10-02: an extended FX speculative short has
more often been the specs being right. **The FX positioning fade class is now
tested empty in both markets we quote. Do not publish it without new evidence.**

## 2. Treasury bond "standoff" — a window artifact, and no edge anyway

The site printed hedge funds at the 99.6th percentile (least short ever) and
asset managers at the 1.7th in the long bond, the most opposed ever
(opposed_markets, Sep 22). Two findings:

1. **The extreme is manufactured by the 2010-06 ranking window.** The
   futures-only TFF record runs from 2006-06. Ranked against the full record,
   the same Sep 22 readings sit at the 54th (HF) and 44th (AM) percentile —
   mid-range. 2006-2010, the excluded years, are exactly when HF were net long
   and AM net short. Bug #1263.
2. **The honest test finds nothing.** HF >= 85th expanding -> forward 63-session
   30Y yield change: 7 episodes, mean +0bp vs +1bp baseline. AM <= 15th: 3
   episodes. Standoff (both): 2 episodes. All inside the noise or below sample
   minimums.

The Sep 29 report (published Oct 2 evening, not yet in the site feed) already
walks the positioning back: HF -13.4% of OI, AM +23.3%.
