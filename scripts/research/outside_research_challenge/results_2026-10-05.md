# Positioning kill-test — 2026-10-05 (daily Trade Idea session)

Method identical to `trend_vs_positioning.py` (expanding percentiles, min 156
weeks, independent episodes 13 clear weeks apart, forward 63 sessions from
report date + 3, trend split on the 200-day). Session egress blocks
publicreporting.cftc.gov, so the pull ran through the database: `net.http_get`
on the CFTC Socrata API (disaggregated futures-only, code 088691), prices from
`net.http_get` on macrotilt.com/indicator_history.json, backtest in SQL against
both responses. 1,060 weekly reports, 2006-06-13 → 2026-09-29.

## Gold — managed money ≥ 85th is not a publishable long, and it is not even firing

The site banner said gold funds at the 85th percentile, new this week. That is
the 3-year window. Honest basis (Sep 29 report): net +29.6% of OI = **65.6th
percentile of the full record** — mid-range. The same 3-year-vs-full trap as
the euro note (see "Which percentile are we quoting?", 2026-10-02).

Signal as the cross-asset map carries it: funds ≥ 85th expanding → LONG (gold
trends; the old-method row showed +9.68% vs +5.30% at 6m).

| | mean 63s | median | win | n |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conditional | +3.5% | +4.5% | 86% | **7 episodes**, 2009–2024 |
| Unconditional | +2.6% | +2.5% | 62% | 892 |

Episodes: 2009-06-02 +4.5%, 2010-04-27 +0.1%, 2011-03-29 +5.6%, 2016-05-03
+5.4%, 2017-08-15 +0.4%, 2019-08-06 −2.9%, 2024-06-11 +11.3%.

Three failures, each sufficient alone:

1. **7 independent episodes** — below the contract's 10-episode minimum for a
   positioning edge.
2. **~0.9pp over baseline** at the mean, nothing like an edge that reaches 15%
   at twice the stop in any listed vehicle.
3. **Every episode had price above its 200-day.** Gold today is 21.6% below
   its January high ($5,318 → $4,172) and under its 200-day ($4,552). No
   tested episode matches the current regime, in either direction.

The cross-asset map's gold row ("does NOT fade — gold TRENDS") was measured
the old way, in an uptrend-only sample. The honest version is narrower: the
long-side extreme signal has too few episodes to publish on, and all of its
evidence is from uptrends. The "never fade gold" half still stands — nothing
here licenses a short either (fund length is mid-range, not extreme, and the
short direction has zero tested episodes).

## Crude — a coordinated reserve release has not knocked price down

Checked against the G7/IEA 100M-barrel release (announced Oct 2). Front-month
WTI (`cmdty_oil`) after each prior coordinated release announcement:

| announced | +21 sessions | +63 sessions |
|---|---|---|
| 2011-06-23 (IEA, Libya) | **+9.0%** | −11.5% |
| 2022-03-01 (IEA) | **+4.3%** | +10.9% |
| 2022-03-31 (IEA/SPR) | **+4.9%** | +8.1% |
| 2026-03-11 (IEA, 400M bbl) | **+10.7%** | +3.2% |

Four of four episodes UP a month later, mixed at a quarter. n=4 is context,
not an edge, in either direction — but it kills "short crude on the release"
as a candidate, and it is too thin and too mixed to publish the contrarian
long.

## Ops note for future runs

A `pipeline_health.last_skip_reason` UPDATE whose string literal contains a
**semicolon** hangs the Supabase management-API gateway until the 60s client
timeout (reproduced 6 times; identical text without semicolons applies
instantly). Write skip reasons without semicolons, or append in chunks.
